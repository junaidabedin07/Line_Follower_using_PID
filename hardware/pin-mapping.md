\# Pin Mapping



\## Sensors

\- D2–D9 : Reflective sensor inputs



\## Motor Driver (TB6612FNG)

\- PWMA : PWM Left Motor

\- PWMB : PWM Right Motor

\- AIN1/AIN2 : Left motor direction

\- BIN1/BIN2 : Right motor direction

\- STBY : Enable pin



\## Power

\- Battery: 7.4V

\- Logic: 5V via buck converter
