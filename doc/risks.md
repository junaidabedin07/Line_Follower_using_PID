\# Risks \& Mitigations



\## Risk: Line Loss at High Speed

Mitigation:

\- Line-lost detection logic

\- Directional recovery based on last error



\## Risk: Sensor Noise

Mitigation:

\- Calibration

\- Shielding

\- Stable power regulation



\## Risk: Power Brownout

Mitigation:

\- Buck converter

\- Decoupling capacitors
