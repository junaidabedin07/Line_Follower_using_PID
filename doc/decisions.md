\# Design Decisions \& Trade-offs



\## Sensor Selection

\- Chosen: Reflective IR array (QTR / equivalent)

\- Reason: High resolution, low interference, competition-proven



\## Control Algorithm

\- Chosen: PID

\- Rejected: Threshold-based logic (unstable at speed)



\## MCU

\- Chosen: Arduino Nano

\- Reason: Reliable, sufficient performance, fast development



\## Motor RPM

\- Selected range: 300–500 RPM

\- Reason: Balance between speed and controllability
